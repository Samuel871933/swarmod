# 3D checklist — paste into every agent that creates, imports or renders 3D content (models, scenes, shaders)

Confirm each item before STATUS: done.
1. Reuse first: look in the project's (and the user's) asset library before making or downloading an asset; follow its copy/index rules.
2. Units and axes stated and consistent: metres; Y-up for glTF/three.js, Z-up for Blender/ROS; scale checked against a known object.
3. Origin/pivot where it is used (feet on the ground, hinge on its axis); transforms applied before export.
4. Geometry: normals outward, no flipped faces or non-manifold edges where it matters; triangle budget stated (web: hero < 50k, props < 5k).
5. PBR materials: base colour and emissive in sRGB; normal, roughness, metalness and AO in linear; textures power-of-two, ≤ 2048 px on web unless needed.
6. Export glTF/GLB when possible; run a validator (gltf-validator or the engine's loader) and quote zero errors.
7. Runtime: draw calls and texture memory checked; instancing for repeats; LOD or culling for big scenes; dispose geometries, materials and textures on unmount.
8. Licence and origin of every external asset recorded (CC0, CC-BY with credit, …); no asset with an unknown licence.
9. A screenshot or render of the result from a fixed camera.
